SOR App — how to put it online and install it on phones
=======================================================

The app must be on an HTTPS website once. After that, every phone installs it
like a normal app (icon on the home screen, full screen, works with no signal).
Records are saved on each phone only — nothing is sent to the website.

STEP 1 — Put the folder online (free, one time) — GitHub Pages
  1. Create a free account at https://github.com
  2. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository". Name: sor-app. Choose Public. Click "Create repository".
  3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in EVERYTHING inside this folder
     (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and the icons folder). Click "Commit changes".
  4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)". Click Save.
  5. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your app address appears at the top:
     https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/sor-app/
  (Alternative: ask IT to place this folder on the company intranet — it must be https.)

STEP 2 — Install on each phone
  Android (Chrome):  open the address > tap "Install" in the app (or menu ⋮ > "Install app" / "Add to Home screen").
  iPhone (Safari):   open the address > Share button > "Add to Home Screen" > Add.
  Laptop (Chrome or Edge): open the address > click the Install icon at the right end
                     of the address bar (or menu > "Install SOR App"). It opens in its own window
                     and gets a desktop / Start menu icon.
  Then open SOR App from the home screen icon.

STEP 3 — Use the laptop as the MAIN register
  On the laptop app, tap the team icon (top right) > TEAM OVERVIEW.
  1. Each team member opens Team overview on their phone > "Send my records".
     This saves one file named with their initials and the date.
  2. Copy those files to the laptop (USB cable or shared network folder).
  3. On the laptop: Team overview > "Import team files" > select all files at once.
     Records merge with photos; anything already imported is skipped, newer edits update.
  4. The laptop then shows totals by observer and contractor, weekly trend and
     overdue actions, and the Log Book prints the combined register / PDF reports.

UPDATING THE APP LATER
  Upload the new index.html to the same repository AND change VERSION in sw.js
  (e.g. sor-app-v1.5). Phones get the update the next time the app is opened with signal
  (close and reopen it once).

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Each phone keeps its own records. Use Settings > Export backup regularly,
    and the Log Book for PDF / Excel.
  - Do not uninstall the app or clear Chrome/Safari site data without exporting a backup first.
